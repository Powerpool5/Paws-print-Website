Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

In short: Paws Print stores the pet information you enter. You can save it to your own Google Drive. We cannot view, access, or collect your data.

1. Who we are

Paws Print (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a mobile app for keeping information about your pets. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it.

2. What the app stores

The app stores the pet information you choose to enter, such as your pets’ names and the details and notes you add about them. This information is kept in the app on your device so you can use it.

3. We cannot view or access your data

Your pet information is not sent to us. We do not operate a server that holds it, and we have no ability to view, read, copy, or retrieve it. We do not sell, rent, or share your information, because we never have it.

4. Saving to Google Drive

The app lets you save your pet information to Google Drive. This only happens when you choose to do it.

  • You sign in with your own Google account and approve the access the app asks for.
  • Your information is saved to your Google Drive, which you control. Google’s own Privacy Policy applies to how Google handles files stored there.
  • The app uses this access only to save and restore your Paws Print data. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not transfer it to anyone.

The app’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

5. Your choices and control

  • Edit or delete: you can change or remove pet information inside the app at any time.
  • Delete your Drive copy: delete the saved file from your Google Drive whenever you like.
  • Remove access: you can revoke the app’s access to your Google account in your Google Account permissions settings.
  • Uninstall: removing the app from your device removes the data stored in it.

6. Security

Because your data stays on your device and in your own Google account, its security depends on how you protect them. We recommend using a screen lock on your device and a strong password with 2-step verification on your Google account.

7. Children

Paws Print is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. We have no access to what is entered into the app.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email your-email@example.com.
